Source data for this benchmark

The CSV source dataset used by the H2GIS benchmark implementation is stored externally on Google Drive:

https://drive.google.com/drive/folders/1heUGNfka2ZXR4klVWUm09wyobPu6yobE?usp=sharing

Download the files and place them in the ./csv/ directory before building or running the benchmark.

Required files:
dimdate.csv
dimsmarine.csv
dimsport.csv
dimtime.csv
dimvessel.csv
trippoints.csv
trips.csv
tripzones_polygons5.csv
tripzones_polygons6.csv
